Find and read SCP articles

Free

Number, title, tag, Object Class, length and Wiki score searches are free. History, ratings, reminders, widgets and the observation summary, activity log and reading backlog are free. Favorites: 20; read later: 100.

Premium

Premium removes ads and unlocks highlights, memo editing, text-to-speech, offline saving, folders, memo and personal-record searches, saved searches and new-match alerts, plus detailed observation analysis. Favorites and read-later have no subscription quota (10,000-item safety limit per list). Monthly and yearly plans are available.

Share from one screen

Choose URLs, titles, article text, memos and highlights separately; your choices are kept for next time. Preview before sharing, copying, saving a file or sending to a registered destination. Open the card/post editor from the same screen. Article text is available from the current ready article in the reader; it is not fetched in bulk from Library lists.

Read each archive in its own language. The app and site support 17 languages; Store metadata has additional regional variants. Source pages, credits and licenses remain authoritative.
